Who we are

A foundation built around one student at a time

Samunnathi connects students with the guidance, mentors, and resources they need to make confident decisions about their education and career. We work directly with schools, professionals, and volunteers to close the gap between ambition and access.

Our work

Four ways we support every student

Education Awareness

Helping students and families understand the educational paths and options genuinely available to them.

Career Guidance

One-to-one guidance sessions that help students match their strengths to realistic career directions.

Mentorship

Pairing students with working professionals and alumni who offer ongoing support and perspective.

Educational Resources

A growing library of scholarships, study material, and application resources curated for students.
